These questions ask how two people in a family are related. Read each clue slowly and turn it into a simple family picture before you answer.

## The basic words

| Person | Is your |
|---|---|
| Father's or mother's brother, or an aunt's husband | uncle |
| Father's or mother's sister, or an uncle's wife | aunt |
| Child of an uncle or aunt | cousin |
| Son or daughter of your brother or sister | nephew or niece |
| Parent of your husband or wife | father-in-law or mother-in-law |
| Husband or wife of your child | son-in-law or daughter-in-law |

## Break the sentence from the end

In a photograph puzzle, start from the speaker ("my") and move one step at a time. Replace each phrase with a single person.

*Example:* Pointing to a boy, Rahul says, "His father is my mother's only brother."
- "My mother's only brother" is one person: Rahul's uncle.
- So the boy's father is Rahul's uncle, and the boy is Rahul's cousin.

Words like "only son" can point back to the speaker. If Rahul is a boy, "my father's only son" is Rahul himself.

## Watch the speaker's gender

"My mother's only daughter" is the speaker herself if the speaker is a girl, but the speaker's sister if the speaker is a boy. The same sentence gives different answers, so always check whether the speaker is a boy or a girl.

Also check which way the question asks. "How is A related to B?" means "A is B's what?" If A is B's grandfather, then B is A's grandchild, not the other way round.
