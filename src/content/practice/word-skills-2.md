This set tests how well you know the alphabet and how carefully you read letters and words.

## Letter positions

Learn the positions of a few anchor letters. The rest is quick counting.

| E | J | O | T | Z |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | 10 | 15 | 20 | 26 |

- *n*th letter from the right = letter number **27 − n** from the left.
- *k* places to the right of a letter: add *k* to its position. To the left: subtract.
- Letters **between** two letters = difference of positions − 1.
- Letter midway between two letters = (sum of positions) ÷ 2.

## Worked example

*Which letter is 6th to the right of the 9th letter from the right?*

9th from the right is letter 27 − 9 = 18, which is R. Six to the right: 18 + 6 = 24, which is **X**.

## Letter pairs in a word

For every pair of letters in the word, count the letters between them in the word, then in the alphabet. Check each letter against every letter after it, both forward (B ... D) and backward (D ... B).

## Hidden words

Write the sentence again without spaces and look for the word, letter by letter. Hidden words often run across two words: "a sl**ow l**ift" hides OWL. The letters must be next to each other and in order.

## Sentence order

- The part with a capital letter usually comes first. The part with the full stop comes last.
- Describing words stay next to what they describe.
- Read your final order aloud in your head. It must sound like a natural sentence.
