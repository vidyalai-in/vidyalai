This set tests memory for words: a list, pairs that belong together, and a short set of instructions. Each card is shown briefly and then hidden.

## Methods that work

1. **Group the list.** Twelve loose words are hard. Sort them in your head: things you eat, things that move, things at home. Three groups of four are much easier.
2. **Make a story for the order.** If the order matters, join the words into a silly story: *the kite fell on a mango, which rolled onto a train...* Each word pulls up the next.
3. **Link each pair with a picture.** For "Arjun: chess", picture Arjun moving a giant chess piece. For "Surat: paper kite", picture a kite over the city. A strange picture sticks better than a plain fact.
4. **Number the steps.** For instructions, count them on your fingers and say the key word of each: *room, chart, name, bag, stage*.
5. **Spend the last few seconds testing yourself.** Look away, recall, then check what you missed.

## Worked example

*Card: Neha: tennis. Vikram: drawing.* Picture Neha hitting a tennis ball at Vikram's drawing. Asked "Who liked drawing?", you see the picture and answer Vikram.

## Common traps

- **Near words**: *lion* when the list had *tiger*, *river* when it had *lake*. Recall the exact word.
- **Swapped pairs**: options often take two real hobbies or cities from the card and match them wrongly.
- **Before or after**: read whether the question asks which came *just after* or *just before*.
