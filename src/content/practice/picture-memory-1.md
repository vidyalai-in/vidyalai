Memory questions reward a good **method** more than a good memory. Use your study time to *organise* what you see, not just to stare at it.

## How to study a picture in 45 seconds

1. **Scan in a fixed order.** Left to right, top to bottom, the way you read. This way you don't miss a corner, and you remember where things were.
2. **Name things in your head.** "Clock, four fifteen. Calendar, twelve June. Three birds." Saying it silently turns a picture into words, and words are easier to keep.
3. **Count every small group.** Questions love "how many": books, pencils, birds, notes. Count while you look.
4. **Read every number and word twice.** Times, dates, room numbers and bus numbers are almost always asked about.
5. **Link the odd ones.** Make a silly link: "the *star* mug is *blue*", "bus *27* leaves at *3:40*".

## When answering

- Answer the questions you are sure about first.
- Watch for **swaps** in the options: 12 and 21, 3:40 and 4:30, top and bottom. Test-makers use them on purpose.
- Your first answer is usually right. Change it only if you clearly remember something else.

## How this set works

Each picture has a **Start studying** button. Once the time runs out (or you press "I'm ready"), the picture hides and its questions appear. You can't look again until you submit. On paper, the picture is on its own page, and the questions start on the next page.
