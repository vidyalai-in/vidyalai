## Issues found
- "Score = relevance × recency decay × importance (the Generative Agents formulation)" is wrong. Park et al. 2023 (Section 4.1) min-max normalize recency, importance and relevance to [0, 1] and combine them as a weighted sum, with all α = 1.
- The Sources line repeated the error ("recency × importance × relevance retrieval").
- First-person voice ("I treat memory", "I keep curated context", "I'd measure").
- Stray escapes `\~300–800`, `\~20`, `\>90%`; tables with empty and bolded fake header rows.
- Mem0 and LongMemEval cited without their headline findings.
- No "Thinking at scale" section (relevant remarks were inline).

## Changes made
- Replaced the formula with `score = α_recency · recency + α_importance · importance + α_relevance · relevance`, with normalization, α = 1, decay factor 0.995 per sandbox game hour since last retrieval, 1-10 LLM importance rating and cosine relevance, all from the paper.
- Added a worked example (peanut allergy vs. yesterday's pasta) showing the sum surfaces an old critical memory that a product would bury; arithmetic: 1.97 vs 1.65 (sum) and 0.019 vs 0.057 (product).
- Added Mem0's reported 91% lower p95 latency and >90% token savings vs. full-context; confirmed the paper's ADD/UPDATE/DELETE/NOOP operations in the PDF.
- Added LongMemEval's five abilities and its ~30% accuracy drop for commercial assistants and long-context LLMs.
- Added Anthropic's client-side memory tool (`/memories` directory) as a production example of MemGPT-style memory tools.
- Labeled the 150 ms latency budget as an example; kept all original numbers (300-800 tokens pinned, 10-30k curated, ~20 turns, 0.1-0.5 facts/turn, >90% precision, 1-3k retrieved).
- Added `## Thinking at scale` (deletion as architecture, versioned extraction, separate ownership of write/read paths, org memory).
- Diagram: `diagrams/q12-memory-tiers.mmd` (write path with extract → gate → ADD/UPDATE/DELETE/NOOP → conflict resolution; read path with pinned, hybrid retrieval, memory tools).
