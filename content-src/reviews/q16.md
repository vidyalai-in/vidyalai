## Issues found
- First-person voice throughout ("I treat it", "I calibrate", "I keep re-auditing").
- Pitfall "Ask candidates how they'd detect it, and expect: ..." is interview voice.
- Inline "The principal-level point is..." in the production section.
- Tradeoffs table had an empty header row and a fake bold header row.
- "You need roughly 1,000+ paired comparisons to resolve 3-point differences reliably": at n = 1,000 and p = 0.5 the 95% CI half-width is 1.96 × sqrt(0.25/1000) = ±3.1 points, as wide as the effect. 80% power for 53% vs 50% needs about 2,180 decisive comparisons (one-sample test, z = 1.96 and 0.84).
- "Chatbot Arena" was renamed LMArena (2025) and then Arena (January 2026, arena.ai).
- MT-Bench numbers are 2023-era judges; needed framing as historical.

## Changes made
- Restructured into the AUTHORING section order; added H3 structure.
- Replaced the sample-size claim with a CI table (200/500/1,000/2,000 → ±6.9/±4.4/±3.1/±2.2), the ~2,200-comparison power figure, the tie-rate adjustment (~2,750 prompts at 20% ties), and paired-design variance `sqrt(d/n)` (d = 15%, n = 1,000 → ±2.4 vs ±4.0 unpaired; ~1,300 prompts for 80% power).
- Added kappa worked example (85% prevalence, 90% raw agreement → κ ≈ 0.61) and made the hallucination example explicit (80 failures, 48 caught, 18 false alarms → 95% accuracy, 60% recall).
- Added MT-Bench Table 2 detail: GPT-4 65.0% consistent (35% inconsistent), GPT-3.5 46.2%, Claude-v1 23.8%; plus the abstract's "over 80% agreement, same as human-human".
- Re-voiced the length-bias pitfall as a detection procedure.
- Moved the principal-level point into `## Thinking at scale` and added ownership/versioning notes.
- Cross-linked the injection post for judge-prompt injection.
- Added diagram `diagrams/q16-judge-calibration.mmd`.
