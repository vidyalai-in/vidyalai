## Changes made
- Cited Kalai, Nachum, Vempala and Zhang (2025), arXiv 2509.04664: binary grading makes guessing score better than abstaining; proposed explicit confidence targets where wrong answers cost `t/(1−t)`. Added worked example (t = 0.5 and t = 0.9; a 40%-confident guess scores −0.2 vs +0.4 under binary grading). Avoided the paper's t = 0.75 example, whose stated penalty (2) doesn't match its own formula (0.75/0.25 = 3).
- Added a selective-accuracy worked example (80% → 92% accuracy at 85% coverage; errors 20% → 6.8%), labeled illustrative.
- Softened "Most RAG hallucinations" to "Many".
- Defined citation precision and recall.
- Added `## Thinking at scale` (per-surface thresholds with owners, fixing internal scoreboards, human audit stream, per-component attribution).
- Diagram (optional, included): `diagrams/q14-hallucination-defenses.mmd`.
