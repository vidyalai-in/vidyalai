## Issues found
- Short answer said "roughly 250 msg/s" while the detail said "about 230/s". 20M ÷ 86,400 = 231.5/s, so ~230/s is correct.
- Short answer was a bulleted list; AUTHORING requires a prose TL;DR.
- Prefill was "plus prefill (mostly cached)" with no number. At peak, 700/s × 3K = 2.1M input tok/s; with 70-90% cache reuse, 210k-630k tok/s of real prefill, ≈ 10-29 replicas of compute at 22k tok/s each, largely absorbed by chunked prefill. The ~80-replica total holds.
- "Inference is about 80-90% of total cost" and "GPUs carry ~85% of the cost" are unsourced; reframed as a planning figure.
- Peak output: original said ~280k tok/s (3x the 92.6k average); 750/s × 400 = 300k. Both shown as a range.
- Other numbers verified: 8B output tokens/day; 60B input tokens/day; 750 × 12 s = 9,000 streams; 40 GB/day and 14.6 TB/year; 320 × $2.50 × 24 = $19,200/day = $0.019/DAU/day. Cost per 1K output tokens ($19,200 ÷ 8M = $0.0024) is consistent with the corrected Q21 range ($0.001-0.003).
- "O(n²) over a conversation without caching" kept, but explained in words.
- Links: research.google/pubs/pub40801/ redirects to /pubs/the-tail-at-scale/; docs.anthropic.com prompt-caching redirects to platform.claude.com.
- Tables: fake bold header row, `\<`, `\>` and `\~` escapes; horizontal rules; "Pitfalls interviewers probe".
