## Issues found
- "At a 5% hit rate, adding 30 ms to 95% of requests may be a net loss" was imprecise: with 2 s generations and 50 ms hits, the mean improves by about 68 ms (0.05 x 1,950 - 30), while p50/p95 get 30 ms worse. The trade is about tail latency and SLOs.
- Coaching line "Say so." after the prompt-caching comparison; "I measure"; "Pitfalls interviewers probe"; "Clarify first".
- Hit-rate tracking lived under an ad hoc heading; template needs "How to measure it".
- Tables had a fake bold header row; `τ\_high`, `\<` escapes.
- vCache citation lacked authors.

## Changes made
- Mapped "Hit-rate tracking" and "Expected numbers" into "How to measure it"; turned expected numbers into a table labeled as typical ranges.
- Added a worked example quantifying lookup overhead vs. savings at a 5% hit rate, and rewrote the related pitfall.
- Re-voiced the prompt-caching comparison with the reason (prefix discount applies to every request with no correctness risk) and linked the Q6 post.
- Put scope key, entry schema and lookup event in code blocks; used `τ_high`/`τ_low` in code formatting.
- Added a request-flow diagram (`diagrams/q08-semantic-cache-flow.mmd`).
- No "Thinking at scale": the original has no org/ownership/migration material; its two decision principles (tolerated false-hit rate, isolation over hit rate) sit in Tradeoffs.
