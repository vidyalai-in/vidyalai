## Issues found
- No `How to measure it` section; only a one-line "Targets" bullet at the end of "After the rollback".
- Power math: `16·p(1-p)/δ²` with p = 0.05, δ = 0.01 gives 7,600 per arm; ÷ 0.02 rating rate = 380,000. Correct, but the assumptions (α = 0.05 two-sided, 80% power, Lehr's approximation) were unstated, and the claim that deterministic metrics catch problems "in minutes" had no supporting arithmetic.
- The export mangled "Treat prompts like code ***and*** like config" into broken bold-italic markers.
- `->` in `chat-system@prod -\> v42` was escaped.
- Tables: fake bold header row; horizontal rules; "Pitfalls interviewers probe".

## Changes made
- Wrote `## How to measure it`: a targets table (time to detect < 15 min, time to mitigate < 5 min, propagation < 60 s, zero incompatible rollbacks, escaped regressions), guardrail thresholds as canary-minus-control bounds sized to stage duration, eval-gate quality metrics (slice coverage, gate recall, judge calibration), and rollback drills.
- Added a worked comparison: parse-error doubling from 0.5% to 1% needs ~4,800 per arm (16 × 0.0075 × 0.9925 / 0.005²), reached in ~14 min on a 5% canary of a 10M-responses/day product, vs ~18 h for the thumbs-down check. Supports the original's "minutes vs hours" claim with numbers.
- Linked version stamping to the OTel GenAI `gen_ai.prompt.name` / `gen_ai.prompt.version` attributes (verified in the semconv registry) and to the tracing post.
- Added the GPT-4o sycophancy rollback as a concrete example of evals missing a slice (per OpenAI's follow-up post as summarized by search results).
- Added a "Thinking at scale" section from the original's confounding-changes and ownership points (single change log, alias-flip permissions, per-tenant targeting).
- Added a release pipeline diagram (registry, eval gate, shadow, canary, monitor, auto/manual rollback, config push, remediation).
