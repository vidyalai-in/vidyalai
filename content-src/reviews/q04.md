## Issues found
- "BEIR showed BM25 beating many dense models zero-shot" is accurate for 2021 but stale as a general statement in 2026. E5 (Wang et al., 2022) abstract: "the first model that outperforms the strong BM25 baseline on the BEIR retrieval benchmark without using any labeled data". Added this nuance while keeping the per-domain/per-slice point.
- "ColBERT: 10-100x more storage than single-vector": rephrased as "an order of magnitude or more when uncompressed", and cited ColBERTv2's own figure (6-10x footprint reduction) and PLAID (tens of ms on GPU, up to 140M passages).
- RRF formula and k=60 verified against Cormack et al. (2009: "k = 60 was fixed during a pilot investigation") and Elastic docs (rank_constant default 60).
- BM25 k1=1.2, b=0.75 are the Lucene/Elasticsearch defaults; kept as "commonly".
- Contextual retrieval: 35/49/67% verified (5.7% -> 3.7% -> 2.9% -> 1.9% top-20 failure rate); Anthropic reranked top 150 -> 20. Added.
- "Overlap 80%+ adds little; 30-50% adds several points" and "linear blend wins by 1-3 points" are unsourced heuristics; labeled as rules of thumb / softened.
- Escapes: `rank\_i`, `\~`.
