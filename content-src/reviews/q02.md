## Issues found
- Fake header rows with bold markers in both tables; `\~` and `\+` escapes.
- First-person voice and "What a principal would add" / "Pitfalls interviewers probe" headings.
- Bucket distribution (10-20 / 30-40 / 20-30 / 10-20%) is unsourced and phrased as personal expectation; Barnett et al. give a taxonomy, not a distribution. Reframed as illustrative.
- "Usually more than half land in retrieval and parsing": unsourced and inconsistent with the illustrative distribution (corpus/parsing + retrieval/ranking = 40-60%). Softened to "more often than not the biggest buckets are retrieval and parsing".
- "On 300 queries a 2-point change is usually noise": verified. SE of a 70% accuracy on n=300 is 2.6 points; paired difference with 10% discordant pairs has 95% CI of about +/-3.6 points.
- 2-5 min/case for manual attribution implies 7-17 SME hours per 200 cases; added so readers can budget.
- Original listed "Eval/label" last in the table; diagram places the label check after "gold in context" so a generation failure is only declared once the label is confirmed valid. Matches table order.
