## Issues found
- "For 30k tokens ... 1-3 s on an H100-class GPU for a 70B model": a 70B BF16 model is about 140 GB and cannot fit on one 80 GB H100. Prefill estimate: 2 x 70e9 x 30k = 4.2e15 FLOPs + causal attention about 1.2e15 = 5.4 PFLOPs. At 50% of 989 dense BF16 TFLOPS that is about 11 s on one GPU, about 2.7 s on TP=4, about 1.4 s on TP=8 or TP=4 FP8. So 1-3 s is right for a 4-8 GPU tensor-parallel group, not one GPU. 500 tokens: about 35 ms on TP=4, 20 ms on TP=8 (consistent with "20-50 ms").
- "Running at 60% utilization rather than 85% may double cost per token but cut p99 TTFT by 5x": cost per token scales with 1/utilization, so it is 85/60 = 1.42x. In an M/M/1 model mean wait falls 3.8x (5.67 vs 1.5) and p99 wait about 2.9x; "5x" is not supported as a general figure.
- "Anthropic reports TTFT reductions of up to ~85%" had no source; verified in Anthropic's prompt caching announcement ("latency by up to 85% for long prompts"; 100k-token example 11.5 s -> 2.4 s).
- vLLM link `docs.vllm.ai/en/latest/features/automatic_prefix_caching.html` is served by a 404 handler (`x-served: Proxito-404`) that 302-redirects to the new path; replaced with the stable URL.
