## Changes made
- Wrote `## How to measure it`: release-gate table, slice reporting, statistics, online metrics, offline-online correlation. Step 4 (release gates) folded into it; online flywheel kept as a design step.
- Verified and extended statistics: SE = √(0.85·0.15/500) ≈ 0.016 → ±3.1 pts; unpaired 2-pt detection at 80% power ≈ 5,000 per run; paired McNemar with 6% discordance (illustrative) ≈ 1,170 items.
- Added pass^k worked example (0.9^5 ≈ 0.59, 0.9^8 ≈ 0.43), assuming independent trials.
- Noted judge calibration should use per-class rates on imbalanced labels (supported by Husain's guide).
- Removed the OpenAI Evals link; mentioned the deprecation under "Thinking at scale" as a reason to keep eval assets in your own storage.
- Added τ²-bench (dual-control) and Anthropic's Jan 2026 agent-evals post.
