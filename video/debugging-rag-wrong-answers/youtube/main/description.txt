Your RAG system gives wrong answers. Before you change the prompt or swap in a bigger model, find out where the evidence disappeared. This video walks through a stage-by-stage method: build a replayable failure set, attribute each failing case to the first stage where the gold evidence goes missing, diagnose each bucket (retrieval, ranking, generation, stale documents, hallucinated citations), then fix the biggest bucket and gate future changes on it. It also covers how to tell a real improvement from noise on a small eval set.
