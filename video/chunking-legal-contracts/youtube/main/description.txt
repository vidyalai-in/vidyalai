Fixed 512-token windows split contract clauses mid-sentence, separate carve-outs from the rules they modify, and lose the defined terms and cross-references that give a clause its meaning. This video walks through a structure-aware alternative: parse the contract into a tree, chunk by clause, add a context header to every chunk, resolve defined terms and one hop of cross-references at retrieval time, retrieve small and expand to the parent section, and track amendments so retrieval prefers current terms. It also covers how to prove the choice with a span-level eval built from CUAD or LegalBench-RAG.
