BM25 wins on exact tokens: identifiers, rare terms, names and error codes. Dense retrieval wins on paraphrase, when queries and documents use different words. Their errors are largely uncorrelated, so the production default is hybrid: run both retrievers in parallel, fuse the lists with reciprocal rank fusion (RRF), and rerank. This video covers how each retriever works and where it breaks, learned sparse retrieval (SPLADE) and late interaction (ColBERT), a worked RRF example, the evidence that the gains stack, a decision guide, and how to choose on your own traffic with per-slice recall.
