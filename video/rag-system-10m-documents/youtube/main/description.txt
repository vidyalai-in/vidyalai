How do you build retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) over 10 million documents? This video walks through the whole design: the requirements that change the architecture, the ingestion pipeline, sizing the vector index (from 410 GB down to about 6 GB), the 2.5-second query path with hybrid search and reranking, what it costs, how to measure it, and the mistakes that sink these projects.
