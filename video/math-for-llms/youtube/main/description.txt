To write a single token, Llama 3.1 8B does about 15.5 billion arithmetic operations, and more than 99.9% of them are one calculation: multiply two lists position by position and add up the results. This video builds the math to follow all of it from high-school algebra: scalars, vectors, matrices and tensors, the dot product, matrix multiplication and the one shape rule worth memorizing, and how to count the arithmetic (about 2mkn FLOPs). Then it follows real data through one forward pass of Llama 3.1 8B, from token IDs to 128,256 scores, tracking the shape and cost of every step, checks the "2 FLOPs per parameter" rule of thumb (and where it breaks at long context), and shows how to read einsum strings like "btd,df→btf".
