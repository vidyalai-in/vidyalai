What really happens when a chatbot answers you? A large language model does one thing: it predicts the next token. This video walks through that loop end to end: text becomes tokens, tokens become vectors, a stack of Transformer blocks mixes in context with attention, softmax turns scores into probabilities, and sampling picks one token to append before the whole model runs again. Then where the 175 billion parameters of GPT-3 come from, how next-token training on trillions of tokens builds a model, why a 1.3B instruction-tuned model beat GPT-3, and why the 2017 paper "Attention Is All You Need" changed everything.
